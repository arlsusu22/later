Reminders not firing

Later uses local notifications. In iOS Settings → Later → Notifications, ensure alerts are allowed. One-time reminders must be scheduled at least slightly in the future.

Share extension

If Later does not appear in the share sheet, tap More in the app row and enable Later. The extension uses the same App Group as the main app so saves stay in sync.
