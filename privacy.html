Privacy policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

Summary

Later helps you save links, add notes, and get local reminders. Your saves live on your device (and in the App Group container shared with the Share Extension and widget). We do not operate an account system in the app, and we do not sell your personal information.

Data we process

  • URLs, titles, and optional notes you enter
  • Reminder times and recurrence you choose
  • App preferences (for example intentions and layout) stored locally
  • Technical data iOS may provide to Apple for App Store and crash diagnostics (subject to your device settings)

How we use data

Data is used only to provide Later’s features: displaying your library, syncing between the app and extensions via the App Group, scheduling local notifications, and showing the widget. We do not use your saved links to train models or for advertising in this app.

Third parties

When you open a saved link, the destination website or app may collect information under its own policies. Optional tips use Apple’s In-App Purchase and are subject to Apple’s terms.

Children

Later is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your region). Do not use the app if you are not old enough to consent to data processing where you live.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy. Material changes should be reflected in App Store release notes when possible.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact on our Support page or the email listed on the App Store.
